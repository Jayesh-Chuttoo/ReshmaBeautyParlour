A salon that grew a boutique

Reshma opened one chair in Curepipe in 2014, doing hair for neighbours and for brides who wanted someone to come to the house. Nails came next, then a small rail of clothing near the window, then the watch case by the door.

The salon is still small on purpose. One client at a time, a proper conversation about what you want, and a price agreed before anything starts.

    Payment and returns policy

    Paying

    All prices are in Mauritian rupees. Orders are paid by MCB Juice transfer to the number shown on the payment screen, using the purchase code as the reference. An order is confirmed only after we have checked the transfer.

    Holding your items

    Items are held for 48 hours from the moment the order is placed. After that the order is cancelled and the items go back on sale.

    Exchanges

    Clothing can be exchanged within 3 days, unworn and with tags, when you show the purchase code. Sale items and earrings cannot be exchanged. Watches carry a 6-month warranty on the movement, not on the strap or glass.

    Appointments

    Tell us at least 4 hours before if you cannot come. For home visits we ask for a day's notice so the travel can be re-arranged.
